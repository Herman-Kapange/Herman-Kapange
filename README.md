# Herman Kapange

Senior software engineer and technology consultant based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.

I build fintech, agritech and government systems that keep working on slow networks and basic phones. I have delivered more than twelve projects across East and Southern Africa and the Caribbean, and most of them are still in production.

Through Handisi Digital Solutions Limited I'm building [Farmcore](https://farmcore.io), an operating system for agricultural cooperatives, and [Provender](https://provender.io), a food operations platform for specialty food brands. I also run [Jenga Tech Consulting](https://jengatechconsulting.com), where I offer technical advisory, fractional CTO support and delivery management.

I mostly work in TypeScript, Python, PHP and Elixir, with React, Next.js, Astro, React Native and Flutter on the front end, Node.js, FastAPI, Laravel and Phoenix on the back end, and PostgreSQL and Redis for data. A lot of my work involves USSD and mobile money integrations.

Most client and product code is private, but you can read the case studies at [hermankapange.com/case-studies](https://hermankapange.com/case-studies).
